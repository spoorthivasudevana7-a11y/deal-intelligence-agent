# Deal Intelligence Agent

An AI-powered agent designed to help analyze business deals, extract important information, identify risks and opportunities, and provide structured insights for better decision-making.

## 🚀 Overview

Business deals often involve large amounts of information distributed across documents, emails, reports, and other sources. Reviewing this information manually can be time-consuming and important details may be difficult to identify.

The Deal Intelligence Agent aims to simplify this process by using AI to analyze deal-related information and generate useful, structured insights.

## 🎯 Problem

Deal teams need to quickly understand:

* What is included in a deal
* Important terms and conditions
* Key stakeholders
* Risks and concerns
* Opportunities
* Important deadlines and obligations
* Overall deal context

Manually extracting and organizing this information can require significant time and effort.

## 💡 Solution

The Deal Intelligence Agent uses AI to process deal-related information and transform unstructured information into actionable intelligence.

The agent can be designed to:

* Analyze deal documents
* Extract key information
* Summarize important findings
* Identify potential risks
* Highlight opportunities
* Answer questions about the deal
* Maintain relevant context across interactions
* Present findings in a structured format

## 🧠 Key Capabilities

### 1. Deal Analysis

Analyze information related to a business deal and identify the most relevant details.

### 2. Information Extraction

Extract important entities, terms, dates, stakeholders, financial information, and obligations.

### 3. Risk Identification

Highlight potentially important risks or areas requiring additional review.

### 4. Opportunity Identification

Surface potentially valuable opportunities or positive signals contained in the available information.

### 5. AI-Powered Q&A

Allow users to ask questions about the deal using natural language.

### 6. Structured Deal Summary

Generate a concise overview of the most important information.
